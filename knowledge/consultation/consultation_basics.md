# Consultation Basics — HealthAssist Platform

*This document is fictional and for demo purposes only.*

## What is a Consultation?

A consultation on HealthAssist is a real-time video or audio session between a patient and a verified doctor. Our platform enables quick connections so patients can get professional medical opinions from the comfort of their home.

## How to Book a Consultation

1. Open the HealthAssist app or website.
2. Browse available doctors by specialization, language, or rating.
3. Select a doctor and choose an available time slot.
4. Complete the payment for the consultation fee.
5. At the scheduled time, join the consultation from your dashboard.

## Consultation Duration

Standard consultations are 15–30 minutes. Extended consultations (up to 45 minutes) are available with select doctors for an additional fee.

## Consultation Statuses

- **Scheduled**: Your consultation is confirmed and upcoming.
- **In Progress**: The consultation session is currently active.
- **Completed**: The consultation has been successfully finished.
- **Failed**: The consultation could not be completed (e.g., technical issues or one party did not join).
- **Cancelled**: The consultation was cancelled by the patient or the platform.

## What Happens After a Consultation?

After a successful consultation, you will receive:
- A summary of the consultation in your dashboard.
- Any prescriptions or recommendations from the doctor.
- The option to book a follow-up consultation.

## Cancellation Policy

- Consultations can be cancelled up to 2 hours before the scheduled time for a full refund.
- Cancellations within 2 hours of the scheduled time may incur a 20% cancellation fee.
- No-shows by patients are not eligible for refunds.
- If the doctor cancels or fails to join, patients receive a full refund automatically.

## Technical Requirements

- Stable internet connection (minimum 2 Mbps).
- A device with a working camera and microphone.
- Latest version of the HealthAssist app or a supported web browser (Chrome, Firefox, Safari).

# Technical Issues — Consultation Troubleshooting

*This document is fictional and for demo purposes only.*

## Common Technical Issues During Consultations

### Audio/Video Not Working

1. Check that your browser or app has permission to access your camera and microphone.
2. Close other applications that might be using your camera or microphone.
3. Try refreshing the page or restarting the app.
4. Ensure you are using a supported browser (Chrome, Firefox, or Safari).

### Poor Connection Quality

1. Switch to a stronger Wi-Fi network or use a wired connection.
2. Close bandwidth-heavy applications (streaming, downloads).
3. Move closer to your Wi-Fi router.
4. If on mobile data, ensure you have at least 4G connectivity.

### Consultation Disconnection

If your consultation is disconnected mid-session:
1. Try to rejoin immediately using the "Rejoin" button in the app.
2. If you cannot rejoin within 2 minutes, the consultation may be marked as failed.
3. Contact support for assistance with rebooking or a refund.

### App Crashes

1. Update the HealthAssist app to the latest version.
2. Clear the app cache.
3. Restart your device.
4. If the issue persists, contact support with your device model and OS version.

## Reporting Technical Issues

When reporting a technical issue, please provide:
- Your consultation ID
- A description of the problem
- Your device type and operating system
- Screenshots or error messages (if available)

This helps our technical team resolve your issue faster.

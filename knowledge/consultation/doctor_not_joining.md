# Doctor Not Joining — Support Policy

*This document is fictional and for demo purposes only.*

## Overview

If a doctor does not join a scheduled consultation, HealthAssist takes this matter seriously. Patient time and trust are our top priorities.

## What Happens When a Doctor Doesn't Join?

1. The system waits for the doctor for up to 5 minutes after the scheduled start time.
2. If the doctor has not joined after 5 minutes, the consultation is automatically marked as "failed."
3. The patient receives an in-app notification and an email about the failed consultation.

## Patient Rights

- **Full Refund**: Patients are entitled to a full refund when the doctor fails to join. The refund is processed within 3–5 business days.
- **Priority Rebooking**: Patients can rebook the consultation with the same or a different doctor at no additional charge.
- **Support Ticket**: A support ticket is automatically created for review by our operations team.

## What You Should Do

1. Contact our support team through the app or this chat to report the issue.
2. Provide your consultation ID for faster resolution.
3. Our support team will verify the issue and initiate the refund or rebooking process.

## Escalation

If the issue is not resolved within 24 hours, the case is escalated to a senior support manager. Patients can request human support at any time during the resolution process.

## Doctor Accountability

Doctors who repeatedly fail to join consultations are subject to review and potential suspension from the platform. HealthAssist maintains strict quality standards for all healthcare providers.

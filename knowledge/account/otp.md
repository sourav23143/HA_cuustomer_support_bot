# OTP Issues — Troubleshooting Guide

*This document is fictional and for demo purposes only.*

## OTP Not Received

1. **Wait 60 seconds** — OTP delivery may be delayed due to network congestion.
2. **Check signal strength** — Ensure you have adequate mobile signal.
3. **Check SMS settings** — Make sure your phone is not blocking messages from unknown senders.
4. **Check DND (Do Not Disturb)** — Deactivate DND by sending "STOP DND" to 1909 (India).
5. **Try alternate method** — Request OTP via email or WhatsApp if available.

## OTP Expired

OTPs are valid for 5 minutes. If your OTP has expired:
1. Click "Resend OTP."
2. Enter the new OTP within 5 minutes.
3. Do not use the old OTP — it is no longer valid.

## OTP Invalid / Incorrect

1. Ensure you are entering the most recently received OTP.
2. Check that you are entering the OTP on the correct screen.
3. If you requested multiple OTPs, only the latest one is valid.

## Maximum OTP Attempts Exceeded

For security, you can request a maximum of 5 OTPs per hour. If you exceed this limit:
- Wait 1 hour before trying again.
- If urgent, contact support for manual verification.

## Contact Support

If none of the above solutions work:
- Provide your registered phone number.
- Describe the exact error message you see.
- Our support team will investigate the OTP delivery issue.

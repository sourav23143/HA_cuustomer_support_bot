# Profile Management — User Guide

*This document is fictional and for demo purposes only.*

## Updating Your Profile

You can update the following profile information:

1. **Name**: Go to Settings → Profile → Edit Name.
2. **Email**: Go to Settings → Profile → Edit Email. A verification email will be sent.
3. **Phone Number**: Go to Settings → Profile → Edit Phone. OTP verification required.
4. **Profile Photo**: Go to Settings → Profile → Upload Photo.
5. **Language Preference**: Go to Settings → Language.
6. **City**: Go to Settings → Profile → Edit City.

## Changing Your Phone Number

1. Go to Settings → Profile → Edit Phone Number.
2. Enter your new phone number.
3. An OTP will be sent to the new number for verification.
4. Once verified, your profile will be updated.
5. You will now log in using the new phone number.

## Deleting Your Account

To delete your HealthAssist account:
1. Go to Settings → Account → Delete Account.
2. You will be asked to confirm deletion.
3. All your data will be permanently deleted within 30 days.
4. Any active consultations or pending refunds will be processed before deletion.
5. Account deletion is irreversible.

## Data Privacy

HealthAssist takes your data privacy seriously:
- Your personal information is encrypted and stored securely.
- We do not share your data with third parties without your consent.
- You can download a copy of your data at any time from Settings → Privacy → Download My Data.

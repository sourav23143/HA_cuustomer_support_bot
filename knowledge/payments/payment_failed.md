# Payment Failed — Troubleshooting Guide

*This document is fictional and for demo purposes only.*

## Why Did My Payment Fail?

Common reasons for payment failure:

1. **Insufficient Balance**: Your bank account or wallet does not have enough funds.
2. **Bank Server Issues**: Your bank's payment gateway may be temporarily down.
3. **Incorrect Details**: Card number, expiry date, or CVV entered incorrectly.
4. **Transaction Limit Exceeded**: Your daily transaction limit may have been reached.
5. **Network Timeout**: Slow internet connection caused the payment to time out.

## What Happens When a Payment Fails?

- The consultation booking is **not confirmed** until payment is successful.
- No amount is deducted from your account for a truly failed payment.
- If the amount was deducted but the payment shows as "failed," the amount will be automatically refunded within 5–7 business days.

## How to Retry

1. Go to your consultation booking page.
2. Click "Retry Payment."
3. Try a different payment method (UPI, Credit/Debit Card, Net Banking).
4. Ensure you have sufficient balance and your bank's servers are operational.

## Amount Deducted but Payment Shows Failed?

This is usually a temporary bank-side issue. Steps:

1. Wait 30 minutes — the payment may auto-reconcile.
2. Check your bank statement after 24 hours.
3. If the amount is still deducted after 24 hours, contact our support team with your payment ID and bank transaction reference number.
4. We will initiate a manual reconciliation and refund if necessary.

## Contact Support

Provide the following when contacting support:
- Payment ID
- Consultation ID
- Amount
- Payment method used
- Screenshot of the error (if available)

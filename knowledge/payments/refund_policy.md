# Refund Policy — HealthAssist Platform

*This document is fictional and for demo purposes only.*

## Refund Eligibility

Patients are eligible for a refund in the following situations:

1. **Doctor Did Not Join**: Full refund if the assigned doctor fails to join the consultation.
2. **Platform Technical Failure**: Full refund if a platform-side technical issue prevented the consultation.
3. **Advance Cancellation**: Full refund if the consultation is cancelled at least 2 hours before the scheduled time.
4. **Late Cancellation**: 80% refund if the consultation is cancelled within 2 hours of the scheduled time.
5. **Duplicate Payment**: Full refund if a duplicate payment is detected.

## Non-Refundable Situations

- Patient no-shows (patient does not join the consultation).
- Completed consultations.
- Cancellations after the consultation has started.
- Dissatisfaction with medical advice provided (this should be escalated to the medical quality team).

## Refund Processing Time

- **UPI Payments**: 3–5 business days.
- **Card Payments**: 5–7 business days.
- **Net Banking**: 5–10 business days.

## How to Request a Refund

1. Contact our support team through the app or this chat.
2. Provide your consultation ID and payment ID.
3. Our team will verify eligibility and initiate the refund.
4. You will receive a confirmation email with the refund reference number.

## Partial Refund

In cases of late cancellation, a partial refund of 80% of the consultation fee will be issued. The 20% cancellation fee covers administrative and doctor reservation costs.

## Compensation and Disputes

HealthAssist does not offer additional compensation beyond the refund amount. Requests for compensation (such as travel expenses, lost wages, or inconvenience fees) are not covered under this policy.

If you believe you have a valid dispute, please request human support, and your case will be reviewed by our dispute resolution team.

## Refund Status

You can check the status of your refund in:
- The "Payments" section of your HealthAssist dashboard.
- By contacting our support team with your payment ID.

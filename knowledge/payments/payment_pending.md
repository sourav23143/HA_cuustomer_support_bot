# Payment Pending — Information Guide

*This document is fictional and for demo purposes only.*

## What Does "Payment Pending" Mean?

A "pending" payment status means the transaction has been initiated but not yet confirmed by your bank or payment provider.

## Common Reasons

1. **Bank Processing Delay**: Some banks take a few minutes to confirm UPI or card payments.
2. **Two-Factor Authentication**: Your bank may be waiting for OTP or biometric verification.
3. **Scheduled Payment**: The payment is queued and will be processed shortly.

## How Long Should I Wait?

- **UPI**: Usually confirms within 2–5 minutes. Wait up to 30 minutes.
- **Card**: Usually confirms within 1–3 minutes. Wait up to 15 minutes.
- **Net Banking**: May take up to 30 minutes depending on the bank.

## What If Payment Is Still Pending After 30 Minutes?

1. Check your bank account or UPI app for any deduction.
2. If the amount was deducted, contact support with your payment details.
3. If the amount was not deducted, try making the payment again.
4. Do not make multiple payment attempts — wait for the first one to resolve.

## Will My Consultation Be Confirmed?

Your consultation is only confirmed once payment is successfully processed. If the payment remains pending, the time slot may be released for other patients after 30 minutes.

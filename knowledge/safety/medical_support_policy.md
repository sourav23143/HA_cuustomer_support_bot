# Medical Support Policy — HealthAssist Platform

*This document is fictional and for demo purposes only.*

## Important Notice

HealthAssist is a platform that connects patients with licensed medical professionals. The HealthAssist support team and AI support assistant are NOT medical professionals.

## What Our Support Team Can Do

- Help with booking, cancelling, or rebooking consultations.
- Assist with payment issues and refunds.
- Resolve account and technical problems.
- Provide information about platform policies and features.
- Escalate medical concerns to the appropriate clinical team.

## What Our Support Team Cannot Do

- Provide medical diagnoses.
- Recommend or prescribe medications.
- Interpret medical test results.
- Provide medical advice or second opinions.
- Replace a consultation with a qualified doctor.

## Medical Emergencies

If you are experiencing a medical emergency:

1. **Call 112** (India Emergency Number) immediately.
2. **Call 108** for ambulance services.
3. **Go to the nearest hospital emergency department.**

Do NOT rely on an online platform for emergency medical care.

## Non-Emergency Medical Concerns

If you have non-emergency medical concerns:
1. Book a consultation with an appropriate specialist on HealthAssist.
2. If you need urgent but non-emergency care, use our "Quick Connect" feature to find an immediately available doctor.

## Adverse Events

If you experience an adverse event related to medical advice received through a HealthAssist consultation:
1. Seek immediate medical attention if needed.
2. Contact our support team to report the incident.
3. Your case will be escalated to our clinical quality team for review.
4. HealthAssist takes all adverse event reports seriously and investigates them thoroughly.

## Feedback on Doctors

If you have concerns about a doctor's conduct or medical advice:
1. Use the "Report" feature in your consultation history.
2. Contact our support team with details.
3. All reports are reviewed by our medical quality team.

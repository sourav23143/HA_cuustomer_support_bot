# About HealthAssist Platform

*This document is fictional and for demo purposes only.*

## What is HealthAssist?

HealthAssist is a healthcare technology platform that connects patients with verified doctors for quick, convenient, and affordable online consultations. Our mission is to make quality healthcare accessible to everyone, regardless of location.

## Key Features

- **Quick Doctor Connection**: Get connected with a doctor within minutes.
- **Verified Doctors**: All doctors on HealthAssist are verified medical professionals.
- **Multiple Specializations**: Access doctors across 20+ specializations.
- **Multilingual Support**: Consult in your preferred language.
- **Secure Payments**: Multiple payment options with end-to-end encryption.
- **Prescription Delivery**: Digital prescriptions delivered instantly after consultation.
- **24/7 Support**: Our support team is available around the clock.

## How It Works

1. **Sign Up**: Create a free HealthAssist account.
2. **Find a Doctor**: Browse by specialization, language, or availability.
3. **Book & Pay**: Select a time slot and complete payment.
4. **Consult**: Join the video/audio consultation at the scheduled time.
5. **Follow Up**: Receive prescriptions, book follow-ups, or contact support.

## Supported Platforms

- Android (version 8.0+)
- iOS (version 14.0+)
- Web (Chrome, Firefox, Safari, Edge)

## Coverage

HealthAssist currently operates across major cities in India with plans to expand to tier-2 and tier-3 cities.
